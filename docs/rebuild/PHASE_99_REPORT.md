## Decision

C. The core does not generalise yet.

Two independent defects show up on unseen requests. The lesson brief keeps at most four content words from the raw teacher sentence, and coverage then requires those words in the pupil text. Instructional words, duration slang, and prior-knowledge wording displace the idea to be taught. Separately, when a lesson does finish, the check often asks for a nearby definition rather than the requested thinking.
