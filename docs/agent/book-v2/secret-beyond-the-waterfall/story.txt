The Water That Made Steps

Page 1
On the Waterfall Ledge, Maya reached through the falling water. Her fingers traced a wet, golden foothold into an opening. Leo tested the step with his foot, then held her hand firmly. "It keeps going," Maya said, reaching beyond the spray.

Page 2
Inside the Shining Passage, Leo splashed water from the stream onto a dark step. Gold spread across it. He pressed his foot against its edge before beckoning Maya. "Here. Put your foot where mine is."

Page 3
On the Water Terrace, Maya traced the stream as it branched around a pale bridge. One corner stuck up above the shallow water. Leo scooped a cloudy patch with an empty shell bowl. He tipped out the silt, and the shell floated.

Page 4
Maya and Leo crossed the bridge on the Water Terrace. A blue stone sat snugly in a triangular socket. Maya lifted it and bent to look underneath. Water pressed through the opening, spreading toward the bridge.

Page 5
On the Water Terrace, water rushed from the open socket toward the bridge. Leo braced himself on the last dry foothold and pulled Maya across. Moving water covered the bridge, washing around their ankles. Maya turned toward their homeward steps; the gold was fading.

Page 6
At the Water Terrace's channel rim, Maya gripped the blue stone with both hands. "My hands are shaking, but I'll put it back." Leo slipped his hand beneath hers. "We'll use the shell together."

Page 7
Beside the flooded bridge on the Water Terrace, Leo lowered the stone into the floating shell. He nudged it toward the center. Then he waited until both rims floated level. Maya took the opposite rim without tipping it.

Page 8
Maya stepped onto the submerged bridge on the Water Terrace. The shell floated between them as Leo followed. She pushed forward, and its rim caught on the hidden corner. A crack ran through the shell.

Page 9
The shell split in two on the Water Terrace. Leo caught the blue stone before it sank. Maya reached into the current and grabbed both curved pieces. Together, they stepped onto the ledge beside the open socket.

Page 10
At the Water Terrace's socket, Leo tried lowering the stone into place. It wobbled on silt packed inside by the rushing water. He lifted it clear while Maya probed the silt with a broken shell edge. "This still scoops."

Page 11
At the Water Terrace's socket, Maya scooped out the last silt with the broken shell. She lifted her fingers clear, and Leo pressed the blue stone firmly into place. The stream returned to its old course. Water drained from the bridge, and the steps shone gold again. Both shell pieces rested beside the sealed socket.

Page 12
Back in the Shining Passage, behind the waterfall, Maya passed the loose stones without touching them. She pressed her wet palm against the wall. Leo added a gold handprint beside hers. Two golden hands gleamed above the shining steps.
