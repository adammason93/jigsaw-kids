The Rain That Rose

Page 1
On the waterfall shelf, a dry leaf drifted straight through the water's edge. Maya followed it and found an opening where no water fell. Beside her, Leo tested his footing before moving closer. "It's dry in there," Maya whispered.

Page 2
Inside the mist pathway, beads of water climbed the sunlit wall. Maya followed them upward, with Leo close behind. The passage ended at a high opening. Below it, pale stepping stones led down toward enormous floating leaves, their curled rims holding pools of water.

Page 3
At the bowl-leaf lake, Maya pressed down on a curled rim. Water slid across the leaf's bowl. Then the pool settled, and the leaf floated level again. Leo watched the rim rise beneath her hand while matching leaves towered nearby.

Page 4
In the sunstripe grove, Maya held her wet hand in the light. Droplets lifted from her fingers. She moved her hand into shade, and the droplets fell. Leo leaned closer as she passed her hand through the sunstripe once more.

Page 5
In the sunstripe grove, water rose through the light and lifted the stepping stones. Leo caught Maya's sleeve. "Our steps!" Their route home drifted overhead, too high to reach.

Page 6
Maya crouched by a fallen bowl leaf. Its pool held the high opening, upside down and small. "The way up is in the water," she said. Leo kept the long stem, and they carried the leaf to the lake.

Page 7
They pushed off, and the boat spun into the shade. It dipped. Beside them, a giant leaf tipped in the sunlight and poured out a bright ribbon. The ribbon climbed across the lake.

Page 8
Leo kept the stem in the sunlight so the boat would not dip again. Droplets rose from Maya's open hand and joined the climbing ribbon. She laughed. Leo watched the bright path and did not let them drift.

Page 9
The leaf began to rise. Far below, every bowl held a round pool of light. A bright line ran from the grove to the high opening. The opening shone like a small waterfall.

Page 10
One side of the leaf lifted higher. Maya slid toward the pool in the middle, and Leo moved with her. The bowl grew level. Then the rising rain carried them up through the narrow light.

Page 11
At the stone lip, Leo held the stem steady. The last water in their bowl climbed the blue wall and joined the droplets there. Maya stepped across. "Now you," she said, and Leo came after her.

Page 12
Outside, the waterfall's edge held a picture of the lake. Round leaves shone there, upside down. Maya and Leo sat together and watched. Drops slipped from Maya's sleeve and fell.
