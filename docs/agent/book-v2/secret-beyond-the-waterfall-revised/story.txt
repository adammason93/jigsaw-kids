The Rain That Rose

Page 1
On the waterfall shelf, a dry leaf drifted straight through the water's edge. Maya followed it and found an opening where no water fell. Beside her, Leo tested his footing before moving closer. "It's dry in there," Maya whispered.

Page 2
Inside the mist pathway, beads of water climbed the sunlit wall. Maya followed them upward, with Leo close behind. The passage ended at a high opening. Below it, pale stepping stones led down toward enormous floating leaves, their curled rims holding pools of water.

Page 3
At the bowl-leaf lake, Maya pressed down on a curled rim. Water slid across the leaf's bowl. Then the pool settled, and the leaf floated level again. Leo watched the rim rise beneath her hand while matching leaves towered nearby.

Page 4
In the sunstripe grove, Maya held her wet hand in the light. Droplets lifted from her fingers. She moved her hand into shade, and the droplets fell. Leo leaned closer as she passed her hand through the sunstripe once more.

Page 5
In the sunstripe grove, water rose through the light and lifted the stepping stones. Leo caught Maya's sleeve. "Our steps!" Their route home drifted overhead, too high to reach.

Page 6
Maya crouched beside a fallen bowl leaf in the sunstripe grove. "Let's ride one up with the water." Leo checked its curled rim and kept the long stem attached. He lifted one side; Maya supported the other, and together they carried their leaf-boat away.

Page 7
At the bowl-leaf lake, Leo held the leaf steady while Maya climbed aboard. Then he eased himself in beside her. The boat turned once, then turned again. Instead of entering the rising rain, it spun toward the shallow bank.

Page 8
Near the shallow bank of the bowl-leaf lake, Leo lowered the long stem. Its end caught the bottom, stopping the boat's spin. "Hold it there," Maya said. She helped push against the stem until their leaf pointed straight.

Page 9
Maya began pushing toward the light on the bowl-leaf lake. "Wait—try a small leaf first," Leo said. She stopped, and together they nudged a small floating leaf into the sunlit current. It rose toward the entrance while their boat stayed braced in the shallows.

Page 10
On the bowl-leaf lake, they poled their boat into the current the small leaf had tested. The rising rain lifted one rim higher than the other. Maya slid toward the central pool, and Leo settled beside her. Their boat leveled as it climbed through the narrowing sunlight.

Page 11
At the mist pathway, Leo braced the stem against the broad stone lip. Maya stepped onto the stone and held the stem firmly. "I've got it. Come across." Leo stepped beside her, leaving their leaf-boat resting against the lip.

Page 12
Back on the waterfall shelf, Leo bent to inspect another dry gap. This time, Maya crouched beside him. Neither hurried through. Beside the falling water, their upside-down reflections shimmered.
